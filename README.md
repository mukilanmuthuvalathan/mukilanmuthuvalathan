## MOXFORGE

[Open MOXFORGE ORGANIZATION & INNOVATION](https://moxforge.vercel.app/)

MOXFORGE is an Information, Technology, and Internet organisation uniting applied software and AI venture creation with an open developer ecosystem. Through Moxforge Innovation Labs and Moxforge Community, we build intelligent web platforms, information engines, and cloud infrastructure.

As an innovation and venture-building organisation, MoxForge helps turn ambitious ideas and emerging technologies into meaningful businesses. Bringing together entrepreneurship, technology, and ecosystem collaboration, we support founders as they shape ideas, build companies, and work toward lasting impact.

Founded by Mukilan Muthuvalathan, a technology entrepreneur and systems architect dedicated to bridging frontier software research with real-world venture creation, MOXFORGE empowers emerging builders, engineers, and founders to transform bold technical insights into scalable internet platforms and enduring enterprises.
